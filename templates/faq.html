FAQ's...

How does the car auction process work?
Our auctions are simple! Create an account, browse available cars, place your bid, and the highest bidder at the end of the auction wins the car.
Is there a registration fee?
No, creating an account and registering on our platform is completely free.
Are the cars inspected before listing?
Yes, all vehicles go through a thorough inspection and verification before being listed for auction. Each listing includes detailed condition reports.
Are there any hidden charges?
No hidden charges. The buyer only pays the winning bid amount and a small service fee, clearly mentioned before checkout.
How do I contact support if I face an issue?
You can reach our support team via live chat, email, or our contact form available on the website.
Is my payment information safe?
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and secure payment gateways to protect your data.
How do I know if a car is accidental or not?
Our inspection report mentions any accident history, major repairs, or frame damage clearly.
What types of vehicles are listed?
We list used cars, new cars, SUVs, sedans, hatchbacks, commercial vehicles, and even vintage cars.
